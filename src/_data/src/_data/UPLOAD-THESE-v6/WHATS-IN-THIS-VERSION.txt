HOW THE TWO MODES WORK
  Standard = your real brand. Hot pink, cyan, yellow, heavy black type.
  Low      = the same palette, dialled down to pastels, no shadows,
             no animation, more spacing.
  The toggle sits top right on every page. The holding page also says
  "Too much? Turn it down." beside it.

CHANGING COLOURS YOURSELF
  Admin > Brand colours. Two sets of swatches, Standard and Low.
  Change one, publish, and it updates every page at once.
